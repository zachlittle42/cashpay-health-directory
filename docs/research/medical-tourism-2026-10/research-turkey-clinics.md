Where a site blocked automated fetching (HTTP 403 for veraclinic.net, hairtransplantfue.org/ASMED, memorial.com.tr), facts come from search-engine snippets of that site's own pages and are marked "[snippet]". "not published" = not found on the pages read. Nothing here is independently verified unless it comes from the Ministry of Health lists (section 13).

"MoH list: FOUND" below means the facility name/address appears in those PDFs. The lists carry no certificate numbers or dates, only province, facility name, facility type, address, phone.
