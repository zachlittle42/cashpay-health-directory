# Korea price research: Seoul dermatology menus, health-checkup packages, regulator lookups

All pages accessed 2026-10-30. USD figures marked "(conv.)" are my conversion at 1,330 KRW per USD; USD figures without that mark were printed on the source page. "Own site" = provider's own website. "MARKETPLACE" = Creatrip, VisitKorea listing, bank promo, or similar third party. Korean clinics quote cosmetic prices before 10% VAT unless stated; several pages note the foreign-patient VAT refund on cosmetic procedures ended 2026-01-01 (Aive, Relieve, Oganacell, Repic, Reddit guide all say this; not verified against a government source).
